Perspective essays, author blogs, and information channels

  • Simon Willison's Weblog — An English practical blog recording tool trials, behavioral checks, and results. Follow concrete inputs and outputs to see how he judges usefulness.

  • Sebastian Raschka: homepage and articles — English technical articles and implementations on LLM architectures, training, and evaluation, connected to from-scratch projects.

  • Karpathy: homepage — Courses, blogs, projects, and social accounts from the author. X / Twitter

  • LessWrong — A community discussing research thinking, AI, and rationality. Specific essays are on the LessWrong selections page.

  • Jiqizhixin — Chinese technology coverage of papers, projects, and industry. Some material requires a subscription.

  • OpenAI News — Original model, research, and product releases from OpenAI.

  • Anthropic Research — Original research releases, browsable by topic.

  • Huazhe Xu: Looking Back at Embodied AI in 2025 — A Chinese annual perspective, linked from Lumina's year-in-review section.

  • Tianwei Lin: VLA in 2025—the Distance from Demos to Generality — A Chinese annual perspective on embodied VLA, linked from Lumina's year-in-review section.

  • Interview with Jianming Wang: Data as a Core Robotics Problem — A Chinese interview about robot data, offering the interviewee's observations of industry and research.
